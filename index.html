Duckdoku Blast – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Duckdoku Blast ("the game") is published by Little Guy Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles and why. The short version: the game keeps your progress on your own device, does not ask you to create an account, and the only information that leaves your device is what Google's advertising and purchase services need to work, plus anonymous usage statistics that help us improve the game (which you can switch off in Settings).

Information stored on your device

Your progress – level reached, coins, boosters, collection, chests, streaks, settings and similar game state – is saved in a file on your device. It is not uploaded to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it. The game does not ask for, or store, your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information.

Leaderboards and Teams shown in the game are generated on your device for the current version; no scores or names are sent to a server.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded and interstitial ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, coarse location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This data is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and AdMob's data practices.

Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), the game shows a consent form before any ad is loaded and respects your choice. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device's Google settings, and clearing the game's data (or reinstalling it) shows the consent form again.

If you buy the No Ads Pass, the game stops requesting ads.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Google provides the game with a purchase record (product and order identifier) so that the items can be granted and restored on this device. Google's handling of your payment data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics and crash reports

The game sends anonymous usage statistics to GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics Ltd) so we can see which levels are too hard, whether the tutorial is clear and how the in-game economy is balanced. This includes: a random identifier created on your device (it is not your advertising ID and cannot be traced back to you), your device model, operating system and game version, an approximate country derived from your IP address by the service, and gameplay events such as levels started, won or lost, boosters used, ads watched, purchases made and coins earned or spent.

It never includes your name, your advertising ID, anything you type (such as crew chat or your player name), your contacts, your precise location or your payment details. GameAnalytics processes this data under its own privacy policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your agreement first and sends no analytics, and stores no analytics ID, unless you say yes. Everywhere else it is on by default. In every region you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Share usage data; turning it off stops the statistics, discards any that were waiting and deletes the random ID. Agreeing is optional and the game plays the same either way. Google Play may also share aggregated install and crash statistics with us through the Play Console; these do not identify you.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices

  • Reset or limit ad personalisation: device Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Allow or stop anonymous usage statistics: Settings → Share usage data (in the EEA, UK and Switzerland the game asks first).
  • See the consent form again: clear the game's data in device Settings → Apps, or reinstall.
  • Delete all game data: uninstall the game.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling data differently – for example if we add online leaderboards or new kinds of data collection – we will update this page and the date at the top before the change ships.
