How to play

  • Every color block hides exactly one duck-ship. Ships never touch, not even diagonally.
  • The numbers along the edges tell you how many ship cells are hidden in each row and column.
  • Tap once to mark water, double-tap to fire. A wrong shot costs a life.
  • The full rules are in the game under Settings → How to Play.

Purchases

  • Bought something and did not get it? Close and reopen the game, then check again. If it is still missing, email us with the date and the item.
  • No Ads Pass after reinstalling or on a new phone: Settings → Restore Purchases.
  • Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: on iPhone and iPad through reportaproblem.apple.com, on Android through Google Play.

Moving to a new phone

Your progress is saved on your phone. To carry it over, open Settings → Backup & Support → Copy backup code on the old phone and keep the code somewhere safe (a note to yourself works). On the new phone, copy the code and tap Restore from copied code.

Chests

The chance of each chest rarity is shown in the game: tap the ? on the chest tray on the home screen.
