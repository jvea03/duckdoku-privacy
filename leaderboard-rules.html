Duckdoku Blast – Leaderboard Official Rules

Effective: 12 November 2026

No purchase necessary. The leaderboards are free to play and the prizes are in-game coins only, with no cash value. Apple Inc. and Google LLC are not sponsors of, and are not involved in, the leaderboards or their prizes in any way.

1. Sponsor

The leaderboards in Duckdoku Blast ("the game") are sponsored and run by Jeffrey Vea, doing business as Little Guy Games ("we", "us"), the developer of the game. Questions: jvea03@gmail.com.

2. Who can take part

Anyone who plays the game, wherever the game and the leaderboards are offered, and where taking part is not prohibited by law. You take part automatically once you reach level 7 and the leaderboards are switched on. There is nothing to sign up for. To keep your scores off the boards, contact us at the address above and we will remove them.

3. The boards

  • Daily: one calendar day.
  • Weekly: Monday 00:00 to Sunday 24:00.
  • Season: one calendar month.

Times are in your phone's own time zone, so a board ends at different moments for players in different places. Every board starts again from zero when it ends.

4. How you score

You earn anchors each time you win a level, including the Daily Puzzle. You get one anchor for each life you have left, plus a bonus when you win a level on your first try. Your anchors count on that day's, that week's and that month's board at the same time. Players are ranked by their anchors on each board, most first.

Anchors can only be earned by winning levels. Nothing in the game can be bought that adds anchors directly. Purchases are never needed to take part or to win.

Each board has a maximum score: 800 anchors on a daily board, 4,000 on a weekly board and 15,000 on a season board.

6. Winners and collecting your prize

Final places are read from the leaderboard 13 hours after a board ends, once the board has ended in every time zone. If players have the same score, the order the leaderboard shows at that moment decides their places.

The game checks your place itself, the next time you open it while online after that, and your prize waits on the Home screen for you to collect. The prize goes to the game install that earned it. Uninstalling the game before collecting it, or playing on another device, means the prize cannot be delivered.

7. Fair play

We may remove scores and refuse or take back prizes from anyone who cheats or tries to. That includes using a modified game, tools or automation, exploiting a bug, or interfering with the leaderboards. It also applies to player names that are offensive or that impersonate someone else; you can report a name in the game. Our decisions on these matters are final.

8. Changes and ending the leaderboards

We may change these rules or the prizes, or pause or end the leaderboards at any time, for example if a problem affects their fairness. Changes apply from when they are published on this page. They never take away a prize that has already been awarded, unless it was awarded because of cheating or an error.

9. Your information

Taking part shares your player name, duck picture and anchor scores with other players. The privacy policy explains what is collected, how it is stored and how to have it removed.

10. Liability

We are not responsible for scores or prizes lost because of network problems, device problems or service outages. As far as the law allows, our responsibility to you in connection with the leaderboards is limited to delivering the in-game coins described above. Nothing in these rules limits your rights under the laws of your country.
